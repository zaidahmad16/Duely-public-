# Changelog

## 0.2.0-beta.1 (first beta)
The first installable Duely.

- **Setup:** Brightspace calendar link, your own Google client, an AI of your choice (Duely picks a model that works
  with your key), optional course announcements, notifications.
- **Duely in your Google account:** the sync, announcement reading and alerts run as a script in your own Google
  account every 10 minutes, even with your computer off. Free, and nothing goes to Duely's developers.
- **Upcoming:** today, this week and next week; tick things done (they get a ✓ in your calendar).
- **Chat:** what's due, what's next, when your test is; mark things done; answer Duely's questions; the chat is kept
  until you clear it.
- **Timetable and final exams from screenshots** (several terms at once), checked by you before anything is added.
- **Alerts:** desktop notifications, email, and Discord DMs from the Duely bot.
- **Updates:** Windows and the Linux AppImage update themselves; macOS and the .deb show a download link.
